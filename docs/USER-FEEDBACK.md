# 👥 HisabDo User Feedback & Analysis

## 1. Objective

As part of the HisabDo AI/ML Capstone Project, the mobile application was shared with five users to understand their experience and identify areas where Artificial Intelligence could provide meaningful improvements.

Each user was asked to explore the application and provide feedback about usability, useful features, difficult tasks, and potential AI-powered improvements.

## 2. User Testing Process

The five users were asked to explore the HisabDo application and consider common financial-management tasks.

The feedback focused on:
- Ease of use
- Useful features
- Difficult workflows
- Expense management
- Financial information
- Reports
- Customer/Khata management
- Potential AI features

## 3. User Feedback Results

| User | Easy to Use? | Most Useful Feature | Main Difficulty | Preferred AI Feature | Suggested Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| User 1 | Yes | Khata | Entering expenses | Receipt Scanner | Make expense entry easier |
| User 2 | Yes | Dashboard | Finding information | AI Financial Assistant | Add an AI assistant |
| User 3 | Yes | Expenses | Categorizing expenses | Automatic Expense Categorization | Automatically categorize expenses |
| User 4 | Yes | Reports | Understanding reports | Financial Insights | Make reports easier to understand |
| User 5 | Yes | Customer/Khata | Remembering customer payments | Smart Reminders | Add payment reminders |

## 4. Key Findings

### Finding 1 — Users Generally Found the App Easy to Use

All five users answered **Yes** when asked whether the application was easy to use.

```text
5 / 5 users → Yes
```

This suggests that the proposed AI improvements should enhance existing workflows rather than significantly change the application's interface.

### Finding 2 — Expense Management Is an Important AI Opportunity

Two users specifically identified expense-related problems.

- User 1 → Entering expenses
- User 3 → Categorizing expenses

This indicates that expense management is a strong candidate for AI automation.

Potential improvements:
- AI Receipt Scanner
- OCR
- Automatic Expense Categorization
- Intelligent Expense Entry

### Finding 3 — Users Want Easier Access to Financial Information

User 2 identified finding information as a difficulty and selected the AI Financial Assistant as the most useful potential AI feature.

This suggests that users may benefit from being able to ask financial questions directly instead of manually searching through records.

Example:

> "How much did I spend this month?"

The AI could retrieve relevant records and return a concise answer.

### Finding 4 — Users Need Help Understanding Reports

User 4 identified understanding reports as a difficulty and selected Financial Insights.

An AI explanation layer could convert financial reports into simple language.

Example:

> "Your monthly expenses increased by 15%. The biggest increases were in transport and utilities."

### Finding 5 — Customer Payment Reminders Are an Opportunity

User 5 identified remembering customer payments as a difficulty and selected Smart Reminders.

The system could provide:
- Outstanding payment alerts
- Overdue payment reminders
- Customer payment history
- Suggested reminder timing
- Priority customers

## 5. AI Opportunities Identified From User Feedback

## 8. Final Top 2 Selection

### 1. AI Receipt Scanner & Smart Expense Processing

Two users independently identified expense-related difficulties:
- User 1 → Entering expenses
- User 3 → Categorizing expenses

Therefore, an AI-powered receipt scanner combined with automatic expense categorization can directly reduce manual work.

### 2. AI Financial Assistant

User 2 identified difficulty finding information and specifically selected the AI Financial Assistant.

The feature can provide a natural-language interface over existing financial records.

Examples:
- "Who owes me the most money?"
- "How much did I spend this month?"
- "What was my biggest expense?"

## 9. Additional Future AI Features

The feedback also supports:
- AI Financial Insights, based on User 4
- Smart Payment Reminders, based on User 5

These can be considered for later phases of the Day 8–Day 60 capstone.

## 10. Conclusion

The five-user feedback activity provided practical evidence for the AI/ML opportunities identified during the HisabDo product analysis.

All five users found the application easy to use, but they identified specific areas where intelligent automation could improve the experience.

The most important opportunities identified were:
- Easier expense entry
- Automatic expense categorization
- Faster access to financial information
- Easier understanding of reports
- Smart customer payment reminders

Based on both product analysis and actual user feedback, the two highest-priority features selected for the capstone are:

1. AI Receipt Scanner & Smart Expense Processing
2. AI Financial Assistant

These features will be further researched, prototyped, developed, and integrated during the remaining internship period.
